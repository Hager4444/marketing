How the results were built

Approach

Separate prospecting from retargeting

Meta Advantage+ Shopping finds new buyers. Catalog retargeting on Meta and TikTok brings back people who viewed products without buying.

Creator content on TikTok

The "Blogger – 3+ Gift" campaign pairs creator videos with a gift on larger orders. In September it returned 11.7× on 9,730 SAR.

Tight targeting on TikTok

A CPA-bid campaign limited to specific audiences and regions reached 10.1× ROAS on 7,982 SAR in September.

Spend less where returns flatten

Snapchat daily spend came down from about US$380 to under US$100 during September, and ROAS rose from 9.7 in August to 16.75.

Retarget through CRM

The audience are divided into different segments, and each segment receives a daily message to encourage them to make a purchase. And it helps us to get 10X ROAS
